# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Adopters:** students and residents in Indian cities (Delhi first) who adopted one sapling from a plantation drive. On a phone, often outdoors at the sapling, in strong sun or at dusk; Hindi-first, English second.
- **Organisers:** an eco-club, residents' association or municipal drive with tens to hundreds of saplings, checking the drive's survival on a laptop or phone.
- **Judges and visitors:** open a link cold and must understand the product in seconds.

Job to be done: keep each young sapling alive through its first dry months, with the least effort, and know which ones need help today.

## Product Purpose

Neemu turns every sapling into a character with a voice. It works out from real weather when the tree needs water, asks its one adopter in Hindi, accepts a proof photo, lets neighbours confirm it is the same tree, and gives the organiser a live survival register and a 3D grove of the whole drive. Success: saplings asked for water exactly when they need it, and a drive whose saplings survive.

## Brand Personality

Regal, rooted, tender. A Mughal garden (charbagh): order, water channels, shade, patience. Calm authority rather than cheerfulness; the tree speaks with dignity. Emotional goal: pride in caring for something living, and the feeling of tending a royal garden.

## Anti-references

- Kids' game: cartoonish, bubbly, over-playful.
- Generic AI app: every element in a rounded card, pill badges everywhere, pastel SaaS palette, tiny uppercase eyebrows over every section.
- Government portal: dense, flat, form-heavy.
- Gold-and-black "luxury" cliché with gradient text.

## Design Principles

1. **The tree is the sovereign.** Each screen has one living subject (the sapling, the grove); everything else frames it.
2. **Order like a charbagh.** Strong symmetry and axes, few containers, rhythm from spacing and fine lines, not boxes.
3. **Honest numbers, plainly said.** Every figure comes from code and real weather; labels say when something is a replay, a projection or a template.
4. **Hindi first, with dignity.** Devanagari is set as primary type, never squeezed beside English.
5. **Outdoor-legible.** Works in harsh sun on a phone and in a video frame.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark themes; follows the phone's light/dark setting. Respects reduced motion. Status never by colour alone (icon + word). Large touch targets for outdoor use.
